🏛️ Project Architecture & Engineering Design

1. Modular OOP Architecture (Three.js Journey Standard)

The codebase is constructed around clean Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) and strict Separation of Concerns:

  • Experience (Singleton): Central coordinator orchestrating scenes, time, sizing, rendering, and lifecycle.
  • Utils & Managers: Dedicated decoupled managers for Time (delta-time RAF), Sizes (responsive DPI handling), EventEmitter (publish/subscribe bus), AudioManager (Web Audio API synthesis), and Raycaster (3D pointer picking).
  • World & Components: Self-contained modules (Island, AlchemistTower, Waterfall, MagicCrystals, AlchemyCauldron, RuneSanctuary, FloatingClouds, MagicalParticles).

2. Real-Time Dynamic & Interactive Systems

  • Dynamic Day/Night Celestial Cycle: Physically computed Sun and Moon coordinates driving Three.js Sky shader, synchronized directional lights, color temperatures, dynamic FogExp2, and 1,800 twinkling stars.
  • Elemental Alchemy Mechanics: 4 interactive crystals (Ignis, Aquas, Terra, Aether) casting dynamic light, emitting Bezier energy beams, and driving chemical reaction blends in the crucible.
  • Procedural Web Audio API: Custom real-time synthesis of pink-noise wind, waterfall rush, harmonic Solfeggio bell chimes, and bubbling liquid—zero external audio dependencies.
  • Interactive Raycasting & Camera Transitions: Object hover highlights, tooltips, responsive feedback, and GSAP smooth view transitions.

3. Rendering & Performance Optimizations

  • PBR Texturing: Physically-based materials with Color, Ambient Occlusion/Roughness/Metalness (ARM), and Normal maps encoded in modern compressed WebP format.
  • Optimized Shadow Maps: Directional shadows constrained with tightly fitted frustums, PCFSoftShadowMap, and normal bias preventing shadow acne.
  • Resource Management: Automated texture loading with fallbacks and complete GPU resource disposal in destroy().
